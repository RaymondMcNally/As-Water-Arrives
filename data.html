What the model is made of.

Everything on this site is public: no addresses, no individual sale prices and nothing that identifies a household. Coordinates are Irish Transverse Mercator (EPSG:2157); heights are metres above Malin Head (m OD).

The water scenarios

For every point on the ground, the site works out the lowest water level, starting from the sea or from Lough Gill, at which water could spread over the land to reach it. This is a morphological reconstruction on the 2 m ground model. Each building takes the lowest value inside its footprint; the kiosk calls these "Sea level that first reaches it" and "Lough Gill level that first reaches it".

The lake scenario is stopped at the weir at Hyde Bridge, so lake water cannot simply run down the tidal river to the sea. Both scenarios ignore tides, wind, defences, drainage, rainfall and time. They show what lies low and connected, as a basis for discussion. They do not forecast floods. Official flood maps are at floodinfo.ie.
