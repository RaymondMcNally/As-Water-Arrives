# Story screen pictures

The story screen (`screen.html`, on the large monitor) shows a picture for whatever the model is showing.
Put a JPG here with the name below and it is used; until then the screen shows the kiosk's own map of the model.
Captions and credits go in `exhibition/config.json` under `screenImages`.

Landscape, about 1600 × 1200 px, under 500 KB. Only use pictures you have the right to show in public, and credit them.

| File | Shown when | Ideas |
|---|---|---|
| `coast.jpg` | OPW sea flood maps, the story's sea steps | The quays at a high spring tide; a storm surge on the Garavogue |
| `sea.jpg` | Sea level slider | Rosses Point or the harbour at low and high water |
| `lake.jpg` | Lough Gill level | Lough Gill from Hazelwood |
| `opw.jpg` | OPW river flood maps | The Garavogue in flood (dated) |
| `ask.jpg` | A question from the Ask tab | The model itself |
| `niah.jpg` | Architectural heritage | Quay Street, Stephen Street, the Custom House area |
| `power.jpg` | Electricity | A town substation (from the street) |
| `nature.jpg` | Protected nature | Cummeen Strand, Lough Gill |
| `planning.jpg` | Planning permissions | A recent development by the river |
| `cycle.jpg` | Cycle routes | The Garavogue riverside path |
| `buses.jpg` | Town buses | A town bus on the quays |
| `traffic.jpg` | Traffic | Hughes Bridge at rush hour |
| `sewer.jpg`, `watermains.jpg` | The two pipe layers | An outfall on the river, a reservoir |
| `areas.jpg`, `zoning.jpg`, `mon.jpg`, `scheme.jpg`, `relief.jpg`, `grid.jpg` | Those layers | The Abbey, historic maps, the printed tiles |

`welcome.jpg` is not needed: the welcome uses `img/model-overview.jpg`.
