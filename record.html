Record a day of TomTom traffic.

Leave this page open for a full weekday. Every 15 minutes it reads TomTom's live traffic for each main road on the model and keeps the result in this browser. The kiosk's A day in 3 minutes then replays the latest complete weekday.

Recorded days

Each strip is one day, midnight to midnight: the share of the main roads that were slow or queuing in each quarter-hour (darker = more congested; blank = no snapshot). A weekday needs at least 72 of its 96 snapshots to be used.

Keep the computer awake

The recording only runs while this page is open and the computer is awake. The page asks the browser to keep the screen on; also set the Mac never to sleep (System Settings › Energy, or Lock Screen › Turn display off: Never) and keep it plugged in. Don't close the tab.

TomTom key

The key is the one saved on the kiosk staff panel or the Projection page, in this browser. One snapshot uses about 50 tiles, so a whole day is about 4,700 of the free 200,000 a month.

Move a recording

The recording lives in this browser. To use it on the exhibition computer, export it here and import it there (on this page, in the exhibition Chrome profile).
