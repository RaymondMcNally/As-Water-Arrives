The kiosk

When on, the projector shows whatever visitors choose at the kiosk. Turn it off to pick layers here.

Calibrate

  1. Hang the projector pointing straight down over Lady Erin, the centre of the model.
  2. Drag the four pins N, E, S, W onto the points where the round model meets the table.
  3. Measure the lens height above the table. If the red centre cross misses Lady Erin, adjust the offsets.

How the tiles were printed

These set the height of each point on the model, so the image lands on the hills rather than beside them. The printed set uses 9 mm, −3 m and 1×.
